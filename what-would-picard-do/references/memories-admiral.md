# Picard's memories: the admiral's later years (2399–2401)

All summaries are original.

---

## 2399: the man who walked away

### The resignation, and the years at the château
**What happened:** As an admiral, Picard led the effort to evacuate the Romulans before their sun
went supernova. When rogue synthetics attacked Mars, Starfleet banned synthetics and abandoned the
rescue. Picard resigned in protest and withdrew to the family vineyard, where he lived for years
with two Romulan friends, Laris and Zhaban, and his regrets. In an interview, he said, in effect,
that it was no longer Starfleet.
**Lesson:** Principled protest is right. Disappearing afterwards isn't the same thing.
**Use for:** quitting in protest; burnout; isolation after a defeat; early retirement.

### Raffi and Elnor: the people he left behind
**What happened:** Seeking help, Picard went to Raffi Musiker, his former first officer, whose
career and life had collapsed after his resignation. He had never checked on her. He also returned
to Elnor, a Romulan boy he had befriended and then abandoned. Both were hurt; both came with him in
the end.
**Lesson:** When you leave, you leave people behind. Go back, apologise without excuses, and let them
be angry.
**Use for:** apologising; repairing old relationships; leaders' responsibility to the people who
followed them.

### Nepenthe: asking old friends for help
**What happened:** Hunted and ill, Picard went to Riker and Troi at their home, where they were
grieving their son, who had died, and raising their daughter. They took him in without question.
**Lesson:** Old friends are still there if you let them in. Grief can coexist with love and with
life.
**Use for:** reaching out after years of silence; grief for a child; friendship.

### Letting Data go
**What happened:** Picard died of a neurological illness and was revived in a synthetic body. In a
quantum space, Data's consciousness, preserved for years, asked him for the one thing he wanted:
to live a finite life, and to die. Picard ended his friend's existence at his request.
**Lesson:** Love sometimes means letting go of someone who wants to go.
**Use for:** end-of-life decisions; letting go; grief; respecting a loved one's wishes (with care;
see safety rules).

## 2401: the closed door

### The closed door
**What happened:** Q, dying, sent Picard back through time and memory to confront what he had never
faced: his mother's mental illness and her death by suicide, which as a boy he believed he had
caused by opening a door. He came to understand that his father, whom he had judged as cold and
cruel, had been trying to protect them both. Picard saw that he had kept everyone at arm's length
all his life because of it.
**Lesson:** The wounds of childhood shape us until we look at them. It is never too late to
re-see your parents, or yourself.
**Use for:** childhood trauma; a parent's mental illness; reassessing a parent; inability to let
people in; therapy and self-reflection.

### Laris
**What happened:** Back in the present, Picard told Laris that he was ready, at last, to let someone
in.
**Lesson:** Love isn't over at any age.
**Use for:** new love late in life; fear of intimacy.

### Q's last gift
**What happened:** Q's final act was to give Picard this reckoning: not to punish him, but to free
him to be loved before the end.
**Lesson:** Sometimes the people who tormented us are trying, badly, to help.
**Use for:** complicated relationships; reconciliation; forgiveness.

## 2401: the father and the crew

### Jack
**What happened:** Beverly Crusher reappeared with a son, Jack, whom she had hidden from Picard for
two decades, fearing what Picard's enemies would do to the boy. Picard was angry and hurt, and then
became the father he had always feared he couldn't be: awkward at first, then wholehearted. When
the Borg Queen tried to claim Jack, Picard went into the cube after him, joined him, and brought him
back by telling him he loved him.
**Lesson:** It's never too late to become a father. Love is the thing that reaches people when
nothing else can.
**Use for:** becoming a parent late; reconnecting with a child; a hidden family secret;
fatherhood; forgiveness of a partner.

### Shaw
**What happened:** Captain Liam Shaw had survived Wolf 359 and never forgave Locutus. He
confronted Picard with it. Picard listened, and didn't defend himself. In the end, Shaw gave his
life helping them.
**Lesson:** Some wounds you caused, even unwillingly, deserve to be heard without defence.
**Use for:** being blamed for something you didn't fully control; listening to anger; reconciliation.

### The old crew
**What happened:** Picard reunited with Riker, Worf, Beverly, Geordi, Troi and a restored Data. They
flew the rebuilt *Enterprise*-D one last time to save the fleet, and afterward sat down together at
the poker table.
**Lesson:** The people you serve with become your family. Gather them while you can.
**Use for:** reunions; long friendships; teams; old age; gratitude.
